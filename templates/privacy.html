Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect information that you provide directly to us. This includes your email address when you create an account, and any expense data you choose to log — such as amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We do not collect sensitive financial information like bank account numbers or credit card details.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the data you provide solely for the purpose of helping you track and understand your spending patterns. Your expense data is used to generate summaries, charts, and reports within your account. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or financial data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely on our servers with industry-standard encryption and access controls. We implement reasonable security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Third-Party Services

Spendly may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, or other functionality. These third parties may have access to your data only to perform specific tasks on our behalf and are obligated to maintain confidentiality. We do not share your expense data with third parties for their own purposes.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, update, or delete your personal data at any time. You can export your expense data or delete your entire account from your profile settings. If you delete your account, all associated data will be permanently removed from our systems.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us through the application.
